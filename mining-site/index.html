OPERATING DOCTRINE

Evidence before economics.

FOREMAN FIRST

Local safety authority can deny, throttle, pause, or stop work independently of economic preference.

CLAIMS ARE LEASES

Work receives bounded resource authority for a defined interval. A Claim is not ownership of the host.

YIELD IS EVIDENCE

Estimated revenue, benchmarks, accepted work, and payable value are deliberately separate states.

TRUST STAYS OUTSIDE

Mining receives no custody, signing, settlement, or trusted-plane authority merely because work is available.

**“What can I see myself in five minutes?”** → Prospect

## Shared boundary

The public ladder is built around **authorized, public, non-destructive observation and investigation**. A higher price buys more evidence depth and analysis, not hidden authority.

Unless separately agreed and explicitly authorized, the ladder does **not** include credentialed access, private systems, destructive testing, exploit attempts, penetration-test certification, compliance certification, source-code audit, smart-contract audit, persistence, privilege escalation, or security guarantees.
